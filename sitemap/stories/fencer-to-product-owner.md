I grew up competing. Nationally ranked fencer through high school and college — the kind of sport where you read your opponent in real time, make decisions in fractions of a second, and learn that preparation and instinct aren't opposites.

Princeton sharpened that into something else: a Comparative Literature degree that taught me how meaning is made, how language carries weight, and how to think across disciplines without losing rigor.
